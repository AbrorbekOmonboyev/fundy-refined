Scope: whole site, home page first (Persuade); explore, project page, donate dialog and receipt follow in the same world. Phone first; donors young and old equally.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The donation-site standard (GoFundMe, charity: water, Kitabisa, JustGiving) at full craft, with Fundy's proof (checked campaigns, stage-by-stage payouts, public receipts) placed exactly where the donor decides. Refuses novelty metaphors and the navy-heavy institutional look.

OWN-WORLD: White and warm-off-white grounds, near-black ink, Fundy green as the single action and progress colour, one warm amber only for "almost there" moments. Onest everywhere (Latin, Uzbek, Cyrillic). Rounded 16px photo cards, thick rounded progress bars, pill buttons, soft depth only on floating things.

STORY: The visitor sees real causes with real photos, understands that every so'm is checked and reported, picks one close to its goal, and donates in three taps from a phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone: logo + menu; a one-line promise and sub-line; the primary Donate path (browse causes) as a full-width green button with a secondary "Start a fundraiser"; three short proof chips (checked, stage by stage, receipts); the first campaign card begins above the fold.

FORM: canon (category standard), chosen by the user; quality bar GoFundMe, charity: water, Kitabisa, JustGiving. seed:97c4aa13637939e1

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
